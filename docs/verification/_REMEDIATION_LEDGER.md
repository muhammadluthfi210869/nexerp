## 1. Overview & Classification Schema

This ledger records every verified finding, defect, contradiction, and false positive identified during the full forensic audit of the 12 core contracts in `docs/legacy-erp/contracts/`.

### Severity Levels:
- **BLOCKER**: Business behavior can be implemented differently, security/access ambiguity, financial/inventory/compliance deadlock, invalid machine-readable contract, authoritative conflict, state/event mismatch, or data corruption risk.
- **MAJOR**: Meaningful documentation drift, stale metadata, unresolved reference, missing supporting governance, incomplete but non-destructive definition.
- **MINOR**: Naming, readability, polish, or formatting inconsistencies.

### Resolution Types:
- **NORMALIZE_AUTHORITY**: Establish single subject-based ownership.
- **SCHEMA_HARDENING**: Fix database constraints, relations, and precision.
- **WORKFLOW_REPAIR**: Fix deadlocks, transitions, and state lifecycles.
- **LOGIC_CODIFICATION**: Codify missing business rules and invariants.
- **RBAC_TIGHTENING**: Enforce Segregation of Duties and resolve inheritance.
- **CONTRACT_ALIGNMENT**: Synchronize API, screen, and event references.
- **DISMISSED_FALSE_POSITIVE**: Prior audit claim proven false upon source inspection.
